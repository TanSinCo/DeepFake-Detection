# 🧱 PHASE 1 — VIDEO + LANDMARK PIPELINE

---

# 🎯 Goal

Convert raw video into analyzable facial geometry.

---

## ✅ What happens

* load video
* reduce FPS (30 → 15)
* resize frames
* detect face mesh
* extract 478 landmarks

---

## 🧠 Output

```text id="gq0a3l"
Frames: (T, 224, 224, 3)
Landmarks: (T, 478, 2)
```

---

## 🧠 Why important

Landmarks become the foundation for:

* ROI extraction
* blink analysis
* motion analysis

---

# 🧱 PHASE 2 — ROI EXTRACTION

---

# 🎯 Goal

Extract meaningful skin regions.

---

## ✅ What happens

Polygon masks created for:

* cheeks
* chin
* forehead

---

## 🧠 Why important

rPPG only works reliably on stable skin regions.

---

## 🧠 Output

```text id="jlwm1p"
ROI regions
```

per frame.

---

# 🧱 PHASE 3 — rPPG EXTRACTION

---

# 🎯 Goal

Extract biological pulse signal.

---

## ✅ What happens

For each ROI:

* extract mean RGB
* create temporal RGB sequence
* apply CHROM method

---

## 🧠 Output

```text id="jlwm2p"
(T, 5)
```

5 physiological signals.

---

## 🧠 Why important

Deepfakes often fail to preserve:

* pulse consistency
* regional synchronization

---

# 🧱 PHASE 4 — FILTERING + NORMALIZATION

---

# 🎯 Goal

Clean biological signal.

---

## ✅ What happens

* Butterworth bandpass filter
* isolate pulse frequencies
* remove noise
* z-score normalization

---

## 🧠 Why important

Raw signal is noisy and unstable.

---

## 🧠 Output

```text id="jlwm3p"
Filtered rPPG
```

---

# 🧱 PHASE 5 — WINDOW SEGMENTATION

---

# 🎯 Goal

Convert long signal into temporal chunks.

---

## ✅ What happens

Split:

```text id="jlwm4p"
120-frame signal
```

into:

```text id="jlwm5p"
6-second windows
```

---

## Example

```text id="jlwm6p"
Frames 0–89
Frames 45–134
Frames 90–179
```

if overlap used.

---

## 🧠 Why important

LSTM learns sequences, not entire long videos.

---

## 🧠 Output

```text id="jlwm7p"
(window_size, features)
```

---

# 🧱 PHASE 6 — EYE BLINK ANALYSIS

---

# 🎯 Goal

Detect unnatural blinking behavior.

---

## ✅ What happens

Using eye landmarks:

* compute EAR (Eye Aspect Ratio)
* detect blink open/close
* analyze blink timing

---

## Features extracted

* blink count
* blink duration
* blink frequency
* abnormal stillness

---

## 🧠 Why important

Deepfakes often:

* blink unnaturally
* blink too little
* blink inconsistently

---

# 🧱 PHASE 7 — MICRO-MOTION ANALYSIS

---

# 🎯 Goal

Analyze subtle facial movement consistency.

---

## ✅ What happens

Track landmark motion:

* cheeks
* jaw
* eyebrows
* forehead

---

## Motion methods

* Euclidean displacement
* optical flow
* temporal consistency

---

## 🧠 Why important

Deepfakes struggle with:

* coherent facial micro-motion
* stable geometric consistency

---
